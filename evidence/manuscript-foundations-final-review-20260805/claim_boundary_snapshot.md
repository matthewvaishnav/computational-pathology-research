# Manuscript-Foundations Release Claim-Boundary Snapshot

**Snapshot of:** the authoritative repository claim boundary relevant to the
Accountable Neural Aggregation manuscript, as of 2026-08-05.
**Authoritative living boundary:** `CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md` (may evolve; this
snapshot is what this release commits to).

## Central safe claims of this release

1. Corrected paired-scanner experiments support **partial structured separation
   under the tested conditions** and **paired supervision**, not pure biological
   factors, complete independence, scanner invariance, or clinical utility.
2. Under the corrected fixed five-category estimand, **no neural feature-space
   increment** over simple scanner-removal baselines was supported; B64 increased
   scanner recoverability and region retrieval without a corrected category gain.
3. Synthetic accessibility effects did **not** transport to corrected real
   category accessibility; retrieval gain is not biological accessibility.
4. TransnnMIL is an **implemented multibranch whole-slide architecture**; its
   corrected matched-control superiority evaluation is pending, and historical
   fusion/topology numbers are withdrawn.
5. PathologyFL is **implemented research infrastructure** with validated
   integration behavior; it is not a validated multi-center deployment.
6. FAIR-WEIGHTS-H is a **proposed and partially implemented** institutional-
   weighting protocol; consistent performance or fairness superiority is not
   established, and "FAIR" in the name does not mean fairness has been proven.
7. PCam is a **patch-level engineering and evaluation benchmark**; no active
   numerical empirical claim is promoted because the underlying result artifacts
   are not tracked.
8. CAMELYON17 weighting studies are **centralized frozen-feature proxies**, not
   full federated-learning validation.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Any superiority, clinical, deployment, or fairness claim not directly
  supported by a corrected, bound artifact.
- Treating PA-NF as the whole research program.
- Presenting projected or withdrawn numbers as observed results.
