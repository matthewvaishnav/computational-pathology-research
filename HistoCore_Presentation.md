## Slide 5: How DMI Works

### 3-Step Medical Intelligence Process

**Step 1: Hospital Registration**
- Medical tier (cancer center, academic, community)
- Board certifications of pathologists
- Research publications & clinical experience
- Historical diagnostic accuracy

**Step 2: Expertise Calculation**
```
Weight = Base × Tier_Multiplier × Certification_Bonus × Accuracy_Factor
Mayo: 1.0 × 3.0 × 2.5 × 0.96 = 9.65x
Rural: 1.0 × 1.0 × 1.1 × 0.87 = 1.12x
```

**Step 3: Intelligent Aggregation**
- Rare cancers: Expert hospitals dominate
- Routine cases: Democratic weighting
- Specialty matching: Breast experts on breast cases
