# Research Repository FAQ

**Updated:** August 2, 2026

## What is this repository?

It is an independent computational-pathology research and research-engineering
repository. Its central line is Paired-Acquisition Neural Factorization, with
secondary work on whole-slide neural models, institutional source effects,
provenance, identifiability, and experiment infrastructure.

It is not a clinically validated product or production hospital platform.

## What is the current main result?

Under corrected biological-unit-blocked evaluation, the paired-acquisition model
reduces linearly recoverable scanner identity in a tissue-oriented branch while
retaining strong scanner information in an explicit acquisition branch and
preserving same-region retrieval within the registered margin.

This supports partial structured separation under the tested protocols. It does
not prove pure biological factors or clinical benefit.

## Which results are promoted?

The principal promoted records are:

- the 175-fit SCORPION capacity-matched campaign;
- the corrected external canine SCC fixed-estimand audit; and
- the 450-cell canine dimensionality × cross-covariance factorial.

Every additional numerical claim requires a forward-valid evidence release.

## Are the open crossed-target PRs established results?

No. They are synthetic exploratory studies. Smoke completion is an engineering
gate, not a final scientific result, and the studies do not establish
pathology-domain validity, novelty, or causal identification.

## Is the paired affine comparison complete?

No. Its protocol and implementation are prospective. No comparative
harmonization claim is permitted until the full campaign, analysis, provenance,
and evidence package are independently validated.

## Is TransnnMIL validated?

The historical fusion and topology interpretations are withdrawn because of
implementation defects. Repaired implementations exist, but matched reruns
against standalone and controlled fusion baselines are still required.

## Is this clinically deployable?

No clinical deployment claim is made. Historical API, DICOM, FHIR, PACS,
security, privacy, and deployment modules are software prototypes or engineering
artifacts. Their presence does not establish operation in a hospital,
regulatory approval, security certification, privacy compliance, or patient
benefit.

## Is the repository HIPAA compliant, FDA approved, or CE marked?

No such claim is made. These statuses require legal, organizational, operational,
technical, and often external validation beyond repository code.

## Is the work state of the art or proven novel?

No. The public repository does not claim state-of-the-art performance, priority,
novelty, patentability, or universal superiority. Literature summaries are
non-exhaustive and date-bounded.

## What does the PCam result mean?

The documented PCam result is `0.9394` ROC AUC and `0.8526` accuracy on one
official patch-level test split. It is an engineering benchmark, not a
slide-level, patient-level, clinical, threshold-validation, or cross-paper
superiority result.
