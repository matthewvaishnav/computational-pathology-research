## Institutional request

The immediate request is not endorsement of a finished clinical technology. It is guidance on whether Conestoga can support the next stage through one or more of the following:

- faculty technical review and potential supervision;
- sponsorship for a Digital Research Alliance of Canada account;
- continuation as a paid student research project;
- assessment for Mitacs, NSERC College and Community Innovation, OCI, or another suitable funding route;
- introductions to pathology, healthcare, scanner, or medical-technology partners;
- advice on publication strategy and stronger external human-tissue validation.

## Current maturity

The project has completed paired-scanner studies on a human H&E benchmark, cross-backbone transfer across three frozen representation families, an independent five-scanner canine SCC validation, matched-budget pair-allocation controls, and forward-valid provenance infrastructure.

The work remains **research-only**. It does not claim clinical validation, diagnostic performance, complete removal of acquisition artifacts, causal biological factors, or improved patient outcomes.

A locked 450-run bottleneck-dimension by cross-covariance factorial is authorized and engineered but remains part of the active development program. Its completion and preregistered aggregate analysis are expected to strengthen the capacity and regularization evidence; they are not required to establish that the current preliminary program is suitable for faculty review.
