# Roadmap

This roadmap separates:

1. implemented engineering
2. tested systems
3. empirical validation
4. future research

## Near-term

- PCam federated benchmarks
- validation of additional FAIR-WEIGHTS-H modes
- cleaner benchmark reporting

## Medium-term

- Camelyon17 validation
- stronger calibration analysis
- subgroup and fairness evaluation

## Long-term

- real multi-center validation
- clinically meaningful studies
- regulatory pathways where applicable

Research progress should be interpreted by evidence level rather than implementation complexity.
