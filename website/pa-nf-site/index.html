Paired-Acquisition Neural Factorization

A focused representation-learning study using matched acquisitions of the same tissue regions to examine how scanner-associated and tissue-associated information are allocated in frozen pathology embeddings.

Current evidence boundary

Corrected biological-unit-blocked and fold-aware evaluation supports partial structured separation under the tested paired-acquisition protocols. The tissue-oriented representation reduces linearly recoverable scanner identity while retaining descriptive tissue-category structure and same-region retrieval; the acquisition branch retains strong scanner information.

This does not establish biologically pure factors, complete scanner invariance, causal factor recovery, clinical utility, or superiority over the strongest simple scanner-removal baselines.

Current paper scope

  • Paired same-region multi-scanner supervision.
  • Separate tissue-oriented and acquisition representations.
  • SCORPION and multi-scanner canine SCC evidence.
  • Corrected fixed-estimand evaluation and simple-baseline controls.
  • Synthetic identifiability and capacity-allocation studies under explicit synthetic-only boundaries.
  • Negative findings, including no supported neural feature-space increment over the strongest simple corrections.
