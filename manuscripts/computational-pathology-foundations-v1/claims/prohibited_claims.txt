# Prohibited claims in the Accountable Neural Aggregation manuscript
#
# These statements must NOT appear in any active result table, abstract, or
# main-text result sentence unless current corrected evidence directly supports
# them. Historical defective results may be discussed only as motivation for
# remediation or preserved historical context — never as active evidence.

## Performance and superiority claims (prohibited unless corrected evidence)
- "TransnnMIL outperforms TransMIL."
- "TransnnMIL outperforms nnMIL."
- "TransnnMIL improves PANDA grading."
- "PathologyFL improves clinical outcomes."
- "FAIR-WEIGHTS-H proves fairness."
- "FAIR-WEIGHTS-H is better than equal or volume weighting."
- "PA-NF is the best scanner-removal method."
- "PA-NF learns scanner-free biology."
- Any method is "clinically ready."
- Any architecture is "state of the art."
- Any method is "the first ever."

## Scope conflation prohibitions
- Treating Paired-Acquisition Neural Factorization as the only research line.
- Describing all centralized frozen-feature experiments as complete federated-learning validation.
- Describing smoke-tested infrastructure as clinical deployment.
- Describing centralized CAMELYON17 source-weighting as full federated learning.

## Evidence-status prohibitions
- Presenting projected TransnnMIL AUC or QWK numbers (from design documents) as observed results.
- Reusing historical TransnnMIL superiority numbers as repaired-model evidence.
- Listing withdrawn or invalidated artifacts in active result tables.
- Claiming architecture novelty implies empirical superiority.
- Equating the "FAIR" name in FAIR-WEIGHTS-H with proven fairness.
- Claiming feature-space evidence establishes pixel-space scanner translation.
- Claiming feature-space category preservation establishes clinical validity.
- Claiming synthetic accessibility effects transport to real pathology features without a corrected category gain.
- Claiming validated scanner swapping (Layer 2) without verified swap metadata.
- Claiming categorical superiority over all prior work without a current literature review.

## Absolute-priority prohibitions
- Any "first to introduce" or "state of the art" wording before a current
  external literature review verifies it.

## Interpretability prohibitions
- "Perfect disentanglement" or "complete independence" for PA-NF branches.
- "Scanner-free biology" for the PA-NF biological branch.
- "Eliminates scanner bias" as a solved problem.
