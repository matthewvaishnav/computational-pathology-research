\section{Limitations}

The program's claims are bounded by the following limitations, all of which are
reflected in the evidence manifest and claim ledger:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{TransnnMIL:} repaired matched controlled reruns are incomplete;
  historical fusion and topology results are withdrawn; projection numbers in
  design documents are not observed results; adaptive pruning is disabled and
  hierarchical pooling lacks a real-data result; several v2 variants and fusion
  controls have no empirical results.
\item \textbf{Paired acquisition:} there is one labeled paired multi-scanner
  category dataset (canine SCC); SCORPION has no validated category labels;
  evidence is frozen-feature only; validated Layer-2 scanner swapping is absent
  (no verified swap metadata); no pixel-space validation; no clinical
  deployment; the corrected fixed-estimand adjudication supports no neural
  feature-space increment and no synthetic accessibility transport; simple
  baselines are strong; fold counts are limited.
\item \textbf{PathologyFL:} no real multi-center deployment; e2e federated
  tests have dangling imports; secure-aggregation and DP guarantees are gated
  on optional libraries and not independently audited; the FAIR-WEIGHTS-H
  aggregator integration is untested.
\item \textbf{FAIR-WEIGHTS-H:} no proven advantage over simpler baselines;
  several protocol elements (training/validation/monitoring weight separation,
  difficulty adjustment, Shapley-style contribution, subgroup constraints,
  weight-change limits, full fallback modes) are specification-only or partial;
  ``FAIR'' does not imply proven fairness.
\item \textbf{PANDA and CAMELYON17:} simulated institutional sites over cached
  feature subsets; no patient-level endpoint or real hospital mapping; limited
  held-out centers (one CAMELYON17 held-out center); centralized CAMELYON17
  studies are frozen-feature proxies, not full federated learning.
\item \textbf{PCam:} patch-level benchmark only; not clinical validation;
  cross-validation paused; threshold study withdrawn.
\item \textbf{General:} no absolute priority claim is made without a current
  external literature review; synthetic-to-real transport failures are
  documented; all pathology claims are bounded to descriptive endpoints and are
  never clinical endpoints.
\end{itemize}

These limitations are stated in the main text rather than deferred to a
supplement because they materially shape which conclusions may be drawn at each
level.
