\section{The PA-NF pipeline}

The research framework spans three decision levels: acquisition-conditioned representation learning, whole-slide prediction, and institutional aggregation. Equation~\ref{eq:program} summarizes their implemented roles schematically. The organizing connection is the question of \emph{information allocation}; it is not a claim that the reported experiments share one trained set of weights or establish an end-to-end causal benefit.

\subsection{Stage I: paired representation factorization}
For tissue region $r$ acquired under scanner $s$, a frozen visual encoder yields $h_{r,s}$. The factorizer maps this feature to tissue-oriented $b_{r,s}$ and acquisition-oriented $a_{r,s}$ branches. The multi-positive region-contrastive objective constrains same-region agreement; joint same-view reconstruction supplies a retention constraint, and complementary scanner readouts test and steer how acquisition-associated signal remains accessible across the branches. SCORPION supplies the registered human controlled comparison; canine SCC supplies an independent labeled paired-scanner comparison; ScanComp supplies separately reported exploratory frozen-checkpoint retrieval on two physical slides.

\subsection{Stage II: whole-slide aggregation}
A whole slide is represented by a bag $\mathcal{B}_k$ of patch features and, where available, spatial coordinates. TransnnMIL combines global correlation modeling with gated MIL aggregation, with optional topology and hierarchical variants. PANDA provides 10,611 readable Phikon feature bags. The completed seven-architecture PANDA study evaluates the repaired model against matched MIL controls on a frozen confirmation split, distinguishing model design from measured predictive performance.

\subsection{Stage III: institutional aggregation}
PathologyFL implements federated training and institutional weighting controls. Controlled PANDA sites test whether validation-triggered switching can reduce the influence of a dominant site when its labels are perturbed. CAMELYON17/WILDS separately evaluates source weighting on an unseen center using a centralized feature-level proxy. These settings establish specific source-influence contrasts, not real-hospital federated validation.

\subsection{What links the studies---and what does not}
At Stage I, the measured quantity is scanner accessibility subject to tissue-region retrieval. At Stage II, it is slide-level predictive performance under matched architecture controls. At Stage III, it is site-level and held-out-center performance under alternative weighting rules. Each study uses its own data, split, statistical unit, and estimator. The framework connects their scientific questions and software components, but the paper does not claim that a representation improvement caused the observed institutional-weighting gains.

The controlling comparators are equal-capacity neural and strong linear corrections for representations; TransMIL, AttentionMIL and nnMIL-family baselines for whole-slide learning; and FedAvg or sample-proportional weighting for institutional influence. Interpretation is attached to those comparisons rather than inferred from component names.
