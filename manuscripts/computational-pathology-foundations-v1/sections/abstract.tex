\textbf{Abstract.}
I present an end-to-end computational pathology research pipeline spanning data preparation, frozen feature extraction, representation learning, whole-slide multiple-instance learning, spatial tissue modeling, and multi-institutional learning. The system is built as one connected research stack rather than a collection of isolated benchmarks. Pretrained encoders such as Phikon, DINOv2, and ResNet are used where appropriate; the paired-representation methods, whole-slide architectures, federated framework, institutional-weighting logic, training and evaluation pipelines, stress experiments, and release infrastructure are developed within this program.

The pipeline has been exercised across five principal pathology settings. On the full 32,768-example PCam public test split, the patch classifier reached 0.9394 ROC AUC, 0.8526 accuracy, and 0.8507 F1. On SCORPION, Paired-Acquisition Neural Factorization (PA-NF) uses 2,400 images from 480 aligned tissue regions on 48 human H\&E slides scanned by five devices; on the documented DINOv2 protocol, tissue-branch scanner-probe accuracy decreased from 0.7825 to 0.3989 while same-region retrieval remained essentially saturated, and the frozen objective transferred to Phikon and ResNet50. An external multi-scanner canine SCC study adds 805 geometry-qualified complete five-view regions across five scanners. On PANDA, 10,611 readable slide-level Phikon feature bags support whole-slide and federated experiments; the repaired TransnnMIL stabilization grid achieved a mean best validation QWK of 0.8257 at learning rate $10^{-4}$ and a best recorded run of 0.8455. In PANDA dominant-site stress experiments, a frozen detector-switch policy improved global QWK by +0.008009 at 25\% corruption and +0.007482 at 35\% corruption while remaining effectively neutral in the clean regime. On CAMELYON17/WILDS, 455,954 examples across five centers were used for held-out-center studies; equal-client weighting improved center-2 accuracy from 0.8312 to 0.9132 on frozen ImageNet ResNet18 features, and dominant-center downweighting reached 0.9322 with CAMELYON17-trained features.

The principal authored systems are PA-NF for paired scanner-aware representation factorization, TransnnMIL for whole-slide neural aggregation, PathologyFL for pathology-specific federated learning, and FAIR-WEIGHTS-H for bounded institutional weighting. Together they form a pipeline that can move from patches and feature bags to slide-level predictions and simulated multi-site training while exposing scanner, center, and institutional effects as explicit experimental variables. The resulting contribution is not a single model: it is a complete computational-pathology research system for studying how information is represented, aggregated across tissue, and aggregated across institutions.
