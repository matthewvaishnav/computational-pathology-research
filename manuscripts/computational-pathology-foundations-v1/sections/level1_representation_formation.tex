The first aggregation level is representation formation: how patch and
foundation-model representations encode tissue, scanner, stain, acquisition
workflow, site or center, region identity, and dataset artifacts. The program's
central design at this level is Paired-Acquisition Neural Factorization
(PA-NF), which uses \emph{exact same-region paired multi-scanner scans} to train
an explicit tissue-oriented biological branch and an explicit acquisition
branch, together with a decoder and paired cross-reconstruction and consistency
objectives. The program also develops the strong simple baselines, broken-pair
controls, synthetic identifiability studies, and corrected fixed-estimand
evaluation that determine what PA-NF does and does not establish.

\subsection{Paired-acquisition design}

PA-NF is trained on exact same-region pairs: the same biological region imaged
under multiple scanners. Training pairs a source region under one scanner with
the same region under another scanner. The biological branch is encouraged to be
consistent across scanner views of the same region; the acquisition branch is
driven by scanner identity. A decoder reconstructs the target view from the
source biological representation and the target acquisition representation
(crossed reconstruction). Biological variance floors and prototype
regularization shape the branch geometry. The factorizer reads paired region and
scanner metadata only; category or biological labels never enter factorizer
optimization.

\subsection{Datasets}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{SCORPION} \cite{ryu2023scorpion}: 48 original H\&E slides, 480
  aligned regions, five
  scanners, and 2{,}400 image patches, under original-slide-blocked folds.
  SCORPION has no validated biological category labels, so only scanner,
  retrieval, and geometric endpoints are available.
\item \textbf{Multi-scanner canine SCC}: a multi-scanner external dataset with
  seven descriptive tissue categories, evaluated under the corrected fixed
  five-category estimand (Dermis, Epidermis, Inflamm/Necrosis, SCC, Subcutis;
  Bone and Cartilage excluded) with biological-sample-blocked folds, fit-only
  probe standardization, and same-region and same-sample nearest-neighbour
  exclusion.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Corrected empirical evidence}

The corrected July 26 forward-valid evidence release supports \emph{partial
structured separation under the tested conditions}, not pure biological factors,
complete independence, or clinical utility. In the separately versioned SCORPION
capacity-matched campaign, the full PA-NF model completed 175/175 registered fits
and, relative to the equal-capacity two-branch neural control without scanner
objectives, reduced tissue-branch scanner balanced accuracy by 0.3108 with a
fold-aware 95\% interval of $[-0.3346,-0.2858]$, while preserving average and
worst same-region retrieval within the registered 0.02 noninferiority margin and
retaining strong acquisition-branch scanner information. This is a controlled
comparative advantage on the registered SCORPION structured-separation objective.

A different question is answered by the corrected canine fixed-estimand audit.
The versioned adjudication (v2, consuming the exact recovered neural artifacts)
established the current status
\texttt{\seqsplit{complete\_no\_neural\_feature\_space\_increment\_supported}}. Under the
corrected five-category estimand, on the recovered B32/B64 cells:

\begin{itemize}
\item B64 did \emph{not} improve corrected five-category canine category
  balanced accuracy (B64 minus B32 mean $-0.008$, one positive fold).
\item B64 \emph{did} increase scanner recoverability (mean $+0.049$, five of
  five folds) and improve worst-pair and overall region retrieval (mean
  $+0.050$ and $+0.051$, five of five folds).
\item No neural feature-space increment over every simple scanner-removal
  baseline was supported: neither B32 nor B64 achieved a category gain of 0.02
  over all simple baselines in four of five folds, and both violated the
  worst-pair retrieval margin.
\item Simple baselines (centroid/QR, paired linear) are strong: they reach
  category and retrieval levels the neural branches do not, while the neural
  biological branch can reach lower scanner recoverability in some folds.
  Neither a neural increment nor a strict simple-baseline Pareto dominance was
  established on this canine fixed-estimand comparison.
\item Synthetic accessibility effects did not transport: the corrected category
  gain required for transport was not present.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Simple baselines and controls}

The program treats simple baselines seriously. Centroid/QR scanner-subspace
projection, PCA scanner-component removal, and paired linear scanner transforms
are all recomputed from the same frozen arrays under the same candidate pool and
probe family. Oldstyle centroid/QR is the strongest raw scanner-removal baseline
(original frozen-feature scanner probe $\approx 0.85$ reduced to
$\approx 0.20$ at chance in corrected audits). Broken-pair controls preserve
scanner counts and remain within the training fold while deranging region
correspondences, isolating the contribution of true pairs.

\subsection{Controlled advantage and bounded negative comparison}

The SCORPION capacity-matched result and the canine fixed-estimand result are not
contradictory because they test different comparators and endpoints. PA-NF has a
supported controlled advantage over the equal-capacity neural control on the
registered SCORPION structured-separation objective. The corrected canine audit,
however, does not support a stronger claim that the neural representation
outperforms every simple scanner-removal baseline on corrected category and
retrieval endpoints.

PA-NF also produces an explicit, inspectable, and decoder-swappable acquisition
branch and a tissue-oriented branch whose scanner recoverability can be reduced
and whose structure can be bottlenecked. Centroid/QR is strong at blind removal
but provides no structural decomposition. The evidence therefore supports a
bounded comparative claim where the registered control establishes it, while
retaining the negative result where the stronger simple-baseline comparison does
not.

\subsection{Layer-2 boundary}

Validated scanner swapping through the decoder (Layer 2) is \emph{not}
established: verified source-region, target-scanner, and swap-assignment metadata
are absent. Swap assignments are never inferred from filenames, row order, or
scanner labels. The missing-metadata schema is a future data-remediation
specification only. Pixel-space evaluation and clinical interpretation are
prohibited by evidence scope.
